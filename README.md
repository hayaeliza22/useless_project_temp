### Project Description
Petty Bench is an AI-powered virtual court designed to solve problems that absolutely do not need solving.
Have a roommate eaten your snacks? Did someone leave one spoon in the sink? Did your friend start an argument and refuse to admit it? Bring the case to Petty Bench and let an unnecessarily serious AI judge decide.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Humanity has somehow survived without a dedicated court for petty arguments.
People are forced to deal with extremely serious matters such as:

- Who ate the last biscuit?
- Who left the light on?
- Who didn't replace the toilet paper?
- Who started the argument?
- Who took the charger?
- Who left one dirty plate in the sink?
- Who finished the snacks without asking?

These disputes have caused arguments, awkward silences, passive-aggressive messages, and unnecessary investigations.
Clearly, the real problem is the *lack of a specialized justice system for nonsense.*

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We created Petty Bench.
Users submit their completely unnecessary disputes to our virtual judge, who analyzes the complaint and delivers an unnecessarily dramatic verdict.
The AI judge doesn't know what actually happened.
It doesn't visit the crime scene.
It doesn't interview witnesses.
It simply listens to your side of the story and confidently judges everyone involved.
Because apparently, that's all the justice system needed.

### Implementation
For Software:
Petty Bench is implemented as a Next.js web application.
The user enters information about a petty dispute and submits it to the court.
The application sends the complaint to a server-side API, which communicates with Google Gemini.
Gemini analyzes the extremely important case and generates a humorous courtroom-style judgment.
The final verdict is displayed to the user through the Petty Bench interface.
The overall process is:

```text
User submits petty complaint
          ↓
Petty Bench receives the case
          ↓
AI Judge analyzes the complaint
          ↓
Magistrate deliberates
          ↓
Court delivers verdict
          ↓
User receives completely unnecessary justice
